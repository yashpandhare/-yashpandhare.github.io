About

I've been at NiCE for a little over three years, starting as an intern in February 2023, and I'm now a Software Engineer there. Most of that time I've worked on the backend of an analytics product for contact centers: Spring Boot services, Python Lambdas, and a natural-language-to-SQL feature built on AWS Bedrock.

The parts of the job I liked most were debugging (race conditions, dependency conflicts, things that only break under load) and security work. I was the Security Champion for my team, and more recently I've been working on FedRAMP remediation across a group of services on the Cognigy platform.

Open to new backend and GenAI roles, and to full-stack work too.

Some of the things I worked on:

  • Added retrieval over stored conversation history (RAG) to our text-to-SQL service. It removed three or more LLM calls from every request.
  • Reworked our prompt templates and cut token usage by 20-30%. Over time I made 170+ merged changes to the Lambda API and its prompts, including multilingual questions and moving to newer Claude models.
  • Found a write conflict between Redis and DynamoDB that made about 30% of our load-test runs fail. After the fix it was 0%.
  • As Security Champion I ran our Veracode and BlackDuck reviews and got our high and medium findings from 30+ down to zero.
  • FedRAMP (NIST SP 800-53) remediation on the Cognigy platform with 4+ teams and 10+ services: SSRF checks, TLS for Kafka and gRPC, keeping credentials out of logs, audit logging. Mostly TypeScript.
  • Moved our service metrics from CloudWatch to OpenTelemetry and Grafana together with the SRE team, and wrote the alert rules (87 of them, in Grafana Mimir) as a Helm chart deployed through Argo CD.
  • Got a GDPR data-erasure Lambda ready for production: cross-account IAM roles, full unit-test coverage, security scans, alarms, and an operational readiness review with SRE.
  • Connected Snowflake to a Spring Boot service (prepared statements, HikariCP, key-pair auth, retries), ran chaos tests with Gremlin on our Lambdas, and wrote a few Claude Code agents and skills that other teams now use.
